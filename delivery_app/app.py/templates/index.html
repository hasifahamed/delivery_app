Common questions

Which areas do you deliver in?

We cover Thanjavur, Ayyampettai and nearby neighbourhoods. WhatsApp your pickup and drop location to confirm coverage.

How do I place an order?

Use the booking form, or WhatsApp +91 95667 00534 with your pickup, drop and what needs delivering. We confirm price and availability right away.

How long does delivery take?

Most deliveries within 5 km take 30 to 45 minutes. Longer distances take a little more, and you get a live ETA when you order.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) and bank transfer. Payment is collected after delivery.

Can I install this as an app on my phone?

Yes, no Play Store needed. On Android (Chrome), tap the Install app button or the browser menu, then Install app. On iPhone (Safari), tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Are medicines and documents handled safely?

Yes. They get top priority and are handed directly to the recipient.
